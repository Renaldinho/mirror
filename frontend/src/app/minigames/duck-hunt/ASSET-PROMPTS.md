# Duck Hunt asset prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The sprite sheets were generated
against chroma-key backgrounds and converted to transparent RGBA atlases.

## Environments

Every environment used this common prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide 16:9 light-gun arcade game
> environment background. Original, legally distinct retro 16-bit pixel art.
> Exactly the bottom 20% is traversable ground and the top 80% is open sky for
> flying targets. Keep the center 70% visually quiet and put decoration at the
> far edges and bottom. No ducks, birds, dog, hunter, weapon, crosshair, UI,
> text, logo, watermark, or copied game assets.

The eight theme directions were:

- Bare: black-glass night sky and soft amber horizon, with sparse reeds and woodland.
- Mushroom: red-cap mushrooms, moss, ferns, and drifting amber spores.
- Occult: candles, broken stone, thorn branches, sigil-like ground markings, and red haze.
- Space opera: alien woodland, severe generic science-fiction machinery, and red alert lights; no known franchise objects or insignia.
- Grim gothic: ruined arches, brass, red wax seals, and smoke; no known franchise design.
- Pinkie: bubblegum sunset, bows, heart-shaped clouds, and lavender grass.
- Emo: monochrome rainy lake, black reeds, distant motel lights, grainy silver moonlight, and an adult dream-pop album mood.
- Spooky gay: camp graveyard, rainbow ectoplasm, dancing skeleton decorations, and colorful ghosts.

## Sprite atlases

All atlases used this common prompt:

> A 4 by 2 sprite atlas, one consistent subject or effect per cell, crisp
> original 16-bit pixel art, generous padding, no overlap, and no labels, grid,
> text, logo, watermark, or copied game assets.

- Duck on flat `#ff00ff`: wing up, wing level, wing down, hit; falling 1,
  falling 2, fleeing up, fleeing down.
- Dog on flat `#00ff00`: sniff 1, sniff 2, sniff 3, jump 1; jump 2, carrying
  duck, laugh 1, laugh 2.
- Effects on flat `#00ff00`: feathers, muzzle star, hit sparkle, dust; loaded
  shell, spent shell, red miss X, gold medal.
