# Game sounds

`shotgun.wav` is the unmodified `sounds/shotty.wav` recording from
[Gunshot Sounds by Tabasco](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds),
downloaded from `https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sounds.zip`.

Other arcade cues are synthesized in `src/app/minigames/audio.ts`.
